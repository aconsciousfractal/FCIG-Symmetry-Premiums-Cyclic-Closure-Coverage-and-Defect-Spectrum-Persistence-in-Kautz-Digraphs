# Source and attribution boundary

## Imported mathematical background

- Xu, Wu, Huang and Yang (2007): ordinary feedback numbers of Kautz digraphs.
- Xu, Yin, Zhang and Huang (2014): adjacent feedback-vertex constructions for
  Kautz digraphs.
- Younger (1963) and Cheung--Kuh (1974): classical feedback-arc/vertex and
  line-digraph correspondences.
- Goldberg--Karzanov (2004): the elementary antisymmetrization device cited in
  the manuscript; no Kautz-specific theorem is imported.
- Marçais, Elder and Kingsford (2024): adjacent reverse-complement work in a
  different graph family and optimization setting.

The bibliography gives exact metadata and DOI links where available. No
third-party article PDF is redistributed.

## Earlier work by the author

*A Parity Theorem for Reverse-Complement-Invariant Minimum Decycling Sets in
q-ary de Bruijn Graphs* treats literal reverse-complement-invariant ordinary
minimum decycling sets in de Bruijn graphs and proves an existence/parity
criterion. The current manuscript treats length-three Kautz digraphs, permits
an invariance premium over the ordinary optimum, and studies closure coverage
and defect. The earlier work is cited for adjacency only; no theorem is
silently transferred.

## Incomplete primary-source access

The 2015 Yin thesis was available through catalogue/abstract information and
the 2016 Zhang--Xu--Yang--Yin article through issue metadata, but their full
texts were unavailable. The corrected 2016 page range is recorded as
2273--2278, subject to that explicit access limitation.

Therefore the manuscript and repository make no novelty, priority, or
source-completeness claim. A future publication decision requires a renewed
source audit if either full text becomes available.
