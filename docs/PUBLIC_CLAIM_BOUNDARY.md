# Public claim boundary

## Affirmative claims

The manuscript proves, for reverse-complement involutions on length-three
Kautz digraphs:

1. the invariant feedback-vertex premium is exactly `m^2`;
2. complete cyclic-closure coverage holds exactly at one moving block in the
   nonidentity domain, with the identity case treated separately;
3. defect spectra are monotone under the stated moving-block and fixed-letter
   extensions;
4. the six-letter base spectrum is exactly `{0,1,2,3,4}`, and these five
   attained values persist for all `m>=3`, `f>=0`;
5. for every acyclic precedence relation on the line-digraph vertices, defect
   equals the transversal number of the minimal infeasible bad-cell clutter;
6. the displayed ten-relation core forces defect at least four and occurs in
   exactly 24 of 1,759 finite symmetry classes, accounting for 1,152 masks;
7. two certified raw sharp facet adjacencies have defect jump three.

## Explicit nonclaims

The repository does not claim:

- novelty, priority, or exhaustive literature coverage;
- that every later defect spectrum equals the six-letter spectrum;
- a universal upper bound of four or a universal interval theorem;
- a complete six-letter facet graph, its connectivity, or its maximum jump;
- that the ten-relation core classifies all 42 defect-four classes;
- that the forced-cell-plus-matching bound is always exact in the broader
  precedence-poset domain;
- that the displayed nonexact rank-three control is a Kautz predecessor DAG,
  or that it settles tightness in the Kautz predecessor-DAG subclass;
- that finite replay substitutes for the all-parameter written proofs;
- a journal, DOI, submission, preprint deposit, or PDF/UA conformance.

The 2015 thesis and 2016 journal article listed in the bibliography were not
available in full text. Their incomplete source status is a publication and
novelty boundary, not a defect in the reproduced finite mathematics.
