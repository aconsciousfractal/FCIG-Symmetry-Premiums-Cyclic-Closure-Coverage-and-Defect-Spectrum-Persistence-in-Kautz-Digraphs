# Accessibility status

The committed paper uses ISO A4 geometry, declares `/Lang=en-US`, has plain
running pages, embedded fonts, live HTTPS links, and no JavaScript, forms,
attachments, launch actions, local-file links, or encryption.

The PDF is **not claimed to conform to PDF/UA** and does not claim a complete
tagged structure tree. The structured LaTeX source in `paper/main.tex`
preserves theorem environments, headings, equations, tables, labels, and
bibliography for alternative rendering or assistive conversion.

The release checks validate language, geometry, metadata, visible keywords and
MSC, repository link, author placement, font embedding, and absence of active
content. A future tagged-PDF or semantic-HTML edition would require its own
assistive-technology review.
